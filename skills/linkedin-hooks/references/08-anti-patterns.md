# 08 — Anti-Patterns

STATUS: draft (add more as user drops war-story swipes)

A running catalogue of reach-killing and trust-killing mistakes. Each entry: *symptom → why it fails → fix → link*.

## 1. Hook that requires context to understand

- **Symptom**: scroll past, no engagement.
- **Why**: feed readers don't have context; hook must be self-contained.
- **Fix**: rewrite so a cold reader gets the tension in the first line.

## 2. Wall-of-text body

- **Symptom**: low dwell time, no completion.
- **Why**: mobile readers bounce on visual walls.
- **Fix**: one-sentence lines, whitespace between beats.

## 3. Bait-and-switch hook

- **Symptom**: expand rate is fine, engagement is zero.
- **Why**: hook promised something the body doesn't deliver.
- **Fix**: write the hook **after** the body, from the body's biggest payoff.

## 4. Generic advice body

- **Symptom**: mild likes, no comments, no saves.
- **Why**: "Be consistent" / "provide value" / "it's a marathon" tells no one anything.
- **Fix**: replace every sentence with a specific noun/verb/number or delete it.

## 5. Outbound link in the body

- **Symptom**: sharp drop in reach.
- **Why**: platform penalizes traffic leaving.
- **Fix**: link in the first comment; body points to it.

## 6. Over-hashtagging

- **Symptom**: noisy visual, weak reach.
- **Why**: reads as spammy; diluted topical signal.
- **Fix**: 0–3 focused hashtags; strongest one first.

## 7. Vague rhetorical question

- **Symptom**: no answers.
- **Why**: reader can't answer "Ever wondered why…?"; they scroll.
- **Fix**: make the question specific and binary/countable.

## 8. "Humblebrag" confession

- **Symptom**: cringe reactions; follows don't convert.
- **Why**: reader senses status-seeking under the confession.
- **Fix**: confess what actually hurt; end with a lesson for the reader, not an applause line.

## 9. Engagement bait the reader notices

- **Symptom**: comments say "this is bait" or silence.
- **Why**: cost of being noticed > value of bait.
- **Fix**: if the post would work without a CTA, use a soft CTA.

## 10. Stacked CTAs

- **Symptom**: no action taken.
- **Why**: choice paralysis.
- **Fix**: pick one.

## 11. "In today's fast-paced world…" opening

- **Symptom**: instant scroll.
- **Why**: LinkedIn-voice filler signals low-value content.
- **Fix**: cut the first 1–2 lines of almost any draft; the third line is usually the real hook.

## 12. Over-structured, no soul

- **Symptom**: reads as AI-assembled.
- **Why**: too many "1. 2. 3. Here's why." blocks with no voice.
- **Fix**: break the pattern once per post with a single human line.

## 13. Hook that gives away the payoff

- **Symptom**: no expansion.
- **Why**: curiosity loop closed in the hook itself.
- **Fix**: the hook states the *outcome* and hides the *mechanism*, or vice versa.

## 14. "I'm humbled / thrilled / excited to announce…"

- **Symptom**: muted response.
- **Why**: generic announcement tone; no hook.
- **Fix**: lead with the *why it matters to the reader*, not the milestone.

## 15. Misused quote opening

- **Symptom**: low engagement.
- **Why**: someone else's words don't earn *your* attention.
- **Fix**: if you must quote, first line is your own; quote comes later as support.

## 16. Controversy without stakes

- **Symptom**: one-sided comments that rehash.
- **Why**: the take is hot but the reader has no skin.
- **Fix**: tie the contrarian take to a concrete decision the reader makes.

## 17. Ending with "thoughts?"

- **Symptom**: shallow or no comments.
- **Why**: reader has no angle to take.
- **Fix**: ask a specific, answerable question OR end with a strong assertion that invites disagreement.

## 18. Posting then editing heavily

- **Symptom**: reach dies.
- **Why**: edits in the first hour can reset or confuse distribution.
- **Fix**: proof ruthlessly before posting; only fix typos after.

## 19. Too many ideas in one post

- **Symptom**: hook says X, body drifts to Y, CTA asks about Z.
- **Why**: the post has no center.
- **Fix**: one idea per post. Spin the others into their own posts.

## 20. Copying a swipe line-for-line

- **Symptom**: community notices; creator's credibility hit.
- **Why**: LinkedIn readers cross-pollinate; theft is noticed fast.
- **Fix**: always remix (pattern + framework stay, surface changes completely).
