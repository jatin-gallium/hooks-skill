# 07 — CTA and Conversion

STATUS: draft

The CTA (call to action) is the last 1–3 lines of the post. It converts reach into outcome.

## CTA types (ranked by intensity, low → high)

1. **Implicit save** — no CTA; the post's scannable structure invites a save.
2. **Soft-comment CTA** — "Curious what yours is." / "Add yours below." Low pressure.
3. **Hard-comment CTA** — "Comment [word] and I'll send you [X]." Explicit trade.
4. **Share CTA** — "Share this with a [role] who needs it."
5. **Save CTA** — "Save this if you [situation]." Works when content earns it.
6. **Follow CTA** — "Follow for more on [topic]." Mild; place at the very end.
7. **DM CTA** — "DM me '[word]' for [resource/call]." Highest intent, highest friction.
8. **Link CTA** — "Link in the first comment." Reach-costing; use when conversion matters more than reach.

## Choosing a CTA

Match CTA type to post goal:

| Post goal            | Best CTA                         |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Reach / top-of-funnel| Soft-comment or implicit save    |
| Positioning / brand  | Follow CTA at very end           |
| List building        | Hard-comment or DM CTA           |
| Booking calls        | DM CTA                           |
| Product / launch     | Link CTA (accept reach hit)      |
| Evergreen value      | Save CTA                         |
| Community            | Share CTA to a named persona     |

## CTA placement rules

- **End of post.** Never mid-post (breaks flow).
- **New line, short.** One line, two at most.
- **One CTA.** Never stack. Picking two = picking none.
- **Match the voice** of the rest of the post. A polished CTA after a raw post feels off.

## Strength vs. reach tradeoff

- Higher-intensity CTAs (DM, Link) get fewer people through but better-qualified.
- Lower-intensity CTAs (soft comment, save) cost less reach and feed the algorithm.
- Alternate CTA types across your posts so every post has a purpose.

## CTA anti-patterns

- "Agree?" — shallow; algorithm has learned to discount.
- "Let me know your thoughts in the comments below." — noise; cut it.
- Asking for 3 things at once ("like, comment, and share") — converts on none.
- A CTA the post didn't earn ("Save this!" on a thin post).
