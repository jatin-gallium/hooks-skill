### #027 — Rippling's TOFU/MOFU/BOFU measurement framework

**Tags:** #metrics #content-measurement #funnel #framework
**Type:** Framework
**Date:** Jan 23, 2026
**Engagement:** 62 reactions, 22 comments (Score: 530)
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7423017361672192001

Senior Manager of Brand & Content at Rippling said: stop measuring all your content the same way.

Matthew Huo from Rippling laid out a framework.

Top of Funnel (Awareness)
You win when prospects say "I see you everywhere."
Measure: engagement, virality, brand recognition and recall

Middle of Funnel (Engagement)
You win when leads enter your orbit.
Measure: emails captured, cost per lead, pipeline influenced

Bottom of Funnel (Conversion)
You win when demos convert to customers.
Measure: demos scheduled, cost per demo, demo-to-closed-won rate

The insight that hit me: most founders create TOFU content but measure it like BOFU.

50K impressions, zero demos? Not a failure. That's awareness working.

Low reach but 5 qualified calls? That's conversion content doing its job.

Different content, different metrics, different definition of winning.

### #029 — B2B content is a trust-building mechanism (deep essay)

**Tags:** #b2b-trust #trust-index #long-term #brand
**Type:** Essay
**Date:** Oct 25, 2025
**Engagement:** 40 reactions, 16 comments, 2 reposts (Score: 364)
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7381759163720384512

Most people misunderstand what B2B content is for.

They treat it like performance marketing. Post on Monday, book demos by Friday. When that doesn't happen, they assume content doesn't work.

But B2B content isn't a conversion event. It's a trust-building mechanism. And trust, by definition, cannot be manufactured quickly.

What B2B content actually does is build what you might call a Trust Index, a composite of signals that accumulate over time:

Audience activation: the percentage of your posts that generate comments from actual decision-makers in your ICP, not just engagement farmers.

Executive influence: the inbound that signals authority. Podcast invites. Press mentions. Speaking requests. Partnership inquiries.

Conversation quality: what percentage of LinkedIn replies convert into substantive multi-message exchanges and eventually meeting requests.

Perception shift: when prospects start using your language. When they quote your frameworks in their own pitch decks.

Internal enablement: when your sales team pulls your posts directly into their materials. When recruiting uses your content to close candidates.

The companies that win long-term in B2B aren't the ones with the best demand gen tactics. They're the ones prospects already trust before the first sales call. That trust is built in public, one post at a time, over months and years.

### #036 — B2B content marketing engine: Spark Plug → Transmission → Driveshaft

**Tags:** #content-engine #funnel #3-parts #automation
**Type:** Framework
**Date:** Sep 25, 2025
**Engagement:** 16 reactions, 8 comments (Score: 160)
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7368674116687589376

The real goal isn't to make content. It's to build an engine.

An automated system that turns total strangers into paying customers while you sleep.

Here's how it works:

Part 1: The Spark Plug (Top of Funnel) Your only job here is to get noticed. You're not selling, you're starting a conversation with interesting ideas. → Social posts, thought leadership, podcasts.

Part 2: The Transmission (Middle of Funnel) They're listening. Now you prove you're the real deal by solving a small part of their problem for free. You build trust. → Case studies, newsletters, webinars.

Part 3: The Driveshaft (Bottom of Funnel) They trust you. Now you make it an absolute no-brainer to buy. You remove all friction between their problem and your solution. → Testimonials, customer stories, demos.

Stop creating content. Start building an engine.

### #040 — Insight-First sales framework — sell insight not services

**Tags:** #sales #insight-first #persona-report #close-rate
**Type:** Framework
**Date:** Jul 27, 2025
**Engagement:** 11 reactions, 5 comments (Score: 105)
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7349155213934514176

The best clients don't buy what you do. They buy how well you understand their world.

You don't need to sell a service. You need to sell them insight.

Last week, an agency in our beta landed a $3K MRR client. They didn't do it with a fancy slide deck.

They walked into the first meeting and handed the founder a 30-page persona analysis on them. An incredibly deep report our AI generated in 20 minutes.

This isn't just a sales tactic. It's a filter. The best clients see the value instantly. The wrong ones walk away. You save weeks of back-and-forth.
