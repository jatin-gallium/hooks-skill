### #077 — I just killed our prompt engineering team — context > prompts

**Tags:** #product #context #personas #first-principles
**Type:** Hot Take
**Date:** Aug 26, 2025
**Engagement:** 26 reactions, 13 comments, 2 reposts (Score: 264)
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7359287661838159874

I just killed our prompt engineering team.

Because everyone tells you to "just type better," but you're still getting soulless, robotic content that makes you want to throw your phone into the sea.

Here's the truth: you're wrestling with the wrong problem.

It's not about the prompt. It's about the context.

Think about it. The Harry Potter series is over a million words. After one book, you feel Harry's world.

Now, imagine telling an actor, "A wizard boy fights a bad guy with no nose," and expecting an Oscar-worthy performance.

Impossible. They have no context.

This is how most people are using AI. They're giving it a one-sentence summary and expecting a masterpiece.

So we started from first principles. What if we solved the context problem first?

We built a system that gives the AI the entire "book" on a person—their articles, interviews, posts, and worldview.

It doesn't just mimic a voice. It understands their world.

The future of AI isn't about better prompts. It's about insane context.

### #079 — GPT-5 handed a toddler a butcher knife

**Tags:** #ai #context #strategy #chef-vs-toddler
**Type:** Hot Take + Framework
**Date:** Aug 26, 2025
**Engagement:** 24 reactions, 6 comments, 2 reposts (Score: 184)
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7359744383857954816

GPT-5 just handed a toddler a butcher knife.

GPT-4 gave the average user a small utility knife. You could make a mess, but the damage was mostly contained.

GPT-5 hands that same user a razor-sharp butcher knife. The raw power is terrifying.

But the operator is still a toddler.

They don't have the context, the nuance, or the strategic thinking to do anything meaningful with it.

The real problem isn't the power of the tool. It's the thinking behind it.

What the toddler needs isn't a bigger knife. It's the mind of a master chef.

Raw Power (The Knife): What GPT-5 provides. The ability to generate text, fast.

Applied Intelligence (The Chef's Mind): The strategic layer. The high-fidelity persona. The deep understanding of a brand's unique voice.

You don't need a stronger toddler. You need an adult in the room.

A powerful tool without wisdom isn't an advantage. It's a liability.

### #152 — Prompt engineering is being replaced by context engineering

**Tags:** #context-engineering #prompts #rag #agents
**Type:** Hot Take
**Date:** Jul 27, 2025
**Engagement:** 73 reactions, 4 comments, 4 reposts (Score: 413)
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7345539932322525185

Prompt engineering is being replaced by context engineering.

As LLMs get more powerful, we expect them to handle more complex tasks. Developers are now building complex workflows and giving sophisticated tasks to AI agents. Simple prompt engineering isn't enough anymore.

Context engineering is about structuring and passing the right information at the right time, using tools like RAG, memory, history, and state. It breaks down a complex task into smaller steps — each with the context it needs to succeed.

If you're building serious AI apps, this is where your biggest wins will come from.
