# Brief — Personal Narrative Post

Use for founder journey, career pivot, belief-change, life moment, vulnerability posts.

## Pre-fills

- **Best formats**: Punchy+Context (most personal narratives), Stacked sub-variant A (before/after timeline) or B (parallel regret), Dense (for scene-setting cold opens).
- **Angle bias**: Emotional contrast, unexpected cost, before/after gap. Never lead with credentials.
- **Rules to emphasize**: Rule 3 (reader-first framing), Rule 13 (compression), Rule 14 ("wrong" framing), Rule 16 (parenthetical self-correction), Rule 34 ("I used to think X. But…"), Rule 37 (external prediction + personal override), Rule 38 (family/community deviation).
- **Register**: Match author's existing voice. Personal brand creators often lowercase.

## Brief

```
BRIEF: personal-narrative

## Raw material

- The unexpected cost / sacrifice / embarrassment inside the story:
  <<...>>

- The before/after gap (numbers or states, no explanation yet):
  - Before: <<...>>
  - After: <<...>>

- A belief I used to hold but changed (no need to explain how):
  <<...>>

- A family / community / peer norm I deviated from:
  <<...>>

- One concrete scene with time, place, dialogue or sensory detail:
  <<...>>

- A confession I almost didn't include:
  <<...>>

## Audience

- Primary audience: <<B2B | personal-brand | mixed>>
- Niche: <<#tag>>
- The reader's internal question: <<"Have I felt this too?">>

## Author voice

- Register: <<standard | lowercase>>
- Voice notes (dry, warm, irreverent, etc.):
  <<...>>

## Format bias

- Prefer: <<Punchy+Context | Stacked-A | Stacked-B | Dense>>
- Avoid: Dense if the story is emotional without data — reads cold.
- Media attached: <<none | image | video>>

## Rules to emphasize

Rule 3 (reader-first), Rule 13 (compression), Rule 14 ("wrong" frame),
Rule 16 (parenthetical), Rule 34 (belief-change), Rule 37 (external prediction override),
Rule 38 (family/community deviation).

## Constraints

- Do NOT open with "Let me tell you about…" or "I want to share…".
- Do NOT use the Crisis → Sleepless night → Revelation formula (banned in SKILL.md).
- No "2 am" / "that night I couldn't sleep" / "Insane? Maybe."
- No em dashes.

## Output request

Generate 8–10 hooks. Lean toward Punchy+Context. Include at least 1 Stacked-A
(before/after timeline) if the raw material supports it. Recommend top 3.
```

## How to use this brief well

- **Lead with the cost, not the win.** Readers connect to vulnerability before they connect to success.
- **The before/after numbers are gold.** Compress ruthlessly — Rule 13 examples in [`../database/by-rule/`](../database/by-rule/) show 12 years in 22 words.
- **Don't explain the emotion.** "Felt invisible." is stronger than "I felt invisible and it really weighed on me."
- **If the story has a villain (teacher, family, peer group), name it.** Rule 37 hooks are among the highest-converting.
