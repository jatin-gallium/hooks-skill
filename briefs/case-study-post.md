# Brief — Case Study / Receipts Post

Use when you have a specific client win or personal result with numbers.

## Pre-fills

- **Best formats**: Dense (numbers need a full sentence to land), Punchy+Context (number on line 1, context on line 2), Stacked sub-variant E (problem-cost-twist).
- **Angle bias**: Lead with the number AND the surprise. The reader's question is "is this real, and does it apply to me?"
- **Rules to emphasize**: Rule 2 (specific numbers), Rule 4 (reader-first framing), Rule 36 (result credential before lesson), Rule 13 (compression).
- **Register**: Standard for B2B case studies.

## Brief

```
BRIEF: case-study

## Raw material

- The single most impressive number:
  <<...>>

- The constraint / context that makes the number surprising (timeframe, team size, budget, industry):
  <<...>>

- One contrarian detail — something the reader would assume worked but didn't, or assume didn't work but did:
  <<...>>

- The one move that unlocked the result (no methodology dump — one move):
  <<...>>

- What generalizes for the reader:
  <<...>>

## Audience

- Primary audience: <<B2B | founder | sales | marketing>>
- Niche: <<#tag>>
- The reader's question: <<"Can I do this?">>

## Author voice

- Register: <<standard>>
- Voice notes:
  <<...>>

## Format bias

- Prefer: <<Dense | Punchy+Context | Stacked-E>>
- Avoid: Single-Line Bomb — case studies need context.
- Media attached: <<none | screenshot | dashboard>>

## Rules to emphasize

Rule 2 (specific numbers), Rule 4 (reader-first), Rule 13 (compression),
Rule 36 (result credential).

## Constraints

- No fabricated or rounded numbers.
- Do NOT lead with the methodology ("We ran a 12-step process…").
- Do NOT lead with the client name — unless the client name IS the hook.
- No em dashes.

## Output request

Generate 8–10 hooks. At least 2 Dense, 2 Punchy+Context, 1 Stacked-E.
Recommend top 3.
```

## How to use this brief well

- **Put the number AND the constraint in the hook.** "$1.2M in 14 days" is fine. "$1.2M in 14 days, from a founder nobody had heard of 3 months ago" is stronger (see Hook 0025 in [`../database/hooks/`](../database/hooks/)).
- **Bury the methodology.** If the hook is "here's how we did it," you lost the reader.
- **Find the "almost didn't work" beat.** That's the hook's tension.
- **Examples to study**:
  - [`../database/hooks/0028-jake-ward-*`](../database/hooks/) — "13,000+ pages with AI in 3 hours, +466% traffic in 60 days."
  - Rule 2: [`../database/by-rule/rule-02.md`](../database/by-rule/rule-02.md).
