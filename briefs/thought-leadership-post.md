# Brief — Thought Leadership / Opinion Post

Use for hot takes, contrarian claims, industry predictions, positioning pieces.

## Pre-fills

- **Best formats**: Single-Line Bomb (if the take is sharp enough), Punchy+Context (take + context line), Dense (if the take needs a data anchor).
- **Angle bias**: Most contrarian OR most specific claim. "Hot Take:" is a legitimate pre-frame. Never lead with a hedge.
- **Rules to emphasize**: Rule 17 ("Hot Take:"), Rule 22 (period between contrasting sentences), Rule 15 (short bomb + detail line), Rule 36 (result credential).
- **Register**: Standard for B2B; lowercase rarely works here (reads under-confident).

## Brief

```
BRIEF: thought-leadership

## Raw material

- The contrarian / unexpected claim (one sentence, defensible):
  <<...>>

- The mainstream view I'm rejecting (name it precisely):
  <<...>>

- 2–4 reasons or evidence points I'll use in the body:
  <<...>>

- A result credential that earns me the right to say it (revenue, clients, years, specific win):
  <<...>>

- The concrete decision this changes for the reader:
  <<...>>

## Audience

- Primary audience: <<B2B | personal-brand | mixed>>
- Niche: <<#tag>>
- The reader's current (wrong) belief: <<...>>

## Author voice

- Register: <<standard>>
- Voice notes:
  <<...>>

## Format bias

- Prefer: <<Single-Line Bomb | Punchy+Context | Dense>>
- Avoid: Stacked — opinion posts rarely fit.
- Media attached: <<none | image>>

## Rules to emphasize

Rule 17 ("Hot Take:" pre-frame), Rule 22 (period between contrast),
Rule 15 (short bomb + detail), Rule 36 (result credential earns the advice).

## Constraints

- Do NOT use the smarmy "it's not X, it's Y" / "Not X… but Y" construction. HARD BAN (see SKILL.md).
- Do NOT hedge the opening ("I might be wrong but…").
- Do NOT open with "I've been thinking about…".
- No em dashes.

## Output request

Generate 8–10 hooks. At least 1 Single-Line Bomb. At least 1 "Hot Take:"-labeled hook.
Include 1 Rule 36 "credential + lesson" variant. Recommend top 3.
```

## How to use this brief well

- **The take has to be defensible, not just provocative.** If you can't list 2–4 reasons in the brief, don't post it.
- **"Hot Take:" works because it names the format.** The reader pre-consents to the friction.
- **Lead short.** "SEO has been dying since 1997." earns the right to 2,000 words of detail behind it.
- **Earn the take with a result.** "We pay 60 salaries from LinkedIn" → audience gives you credit for the opinion that follows.
