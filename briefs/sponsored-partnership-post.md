# Brief — Sponsored / Partnership Post

Use for paid posts, brand partnerships, affiliate content.

## Pre-fills

- **Best formats**: Punchy+Context with transparency parenthetical, Dense with identity-disclaimer opener.
- **Angle bias**: Pre-empt skepticism INSIDE the hook. Never open with brand/product name.
- **Rules to emphasize**: Rule 25 (identity disclaimer flip — "I'm not a developer, but…"), Rule 31 (transparency parenthetical — "(that aren't just another paid post)").
- **Register**: Match the author's baseline register.

## Brief

```
BRIEF: sponsored-partnership

## Raw material

- The shared identity I have with the reader (non-expert, skeptic, normie, etc.):
  <<...>>

- The disclaimer I'll put in parentheses to pre-empt skepticism:
  <<...>>

- The one thing the product/brand does that I'd actually recommend:
  <<...>>

- A result I personally got from using it (concrete, small is fine):
  <<...>>

- Partnership context the reader deserves to know:
  <<...>>

## Audience

- Primary audience: <<B2B | personal-brand | mixed>>
- Niche: <<#tag>>
- What the reader is skeptical of: <<"another paid post", "influencer shilling">>

## Author voice

- Register: <<standard | lowercase>>
- Voice notes:
  <<...>>

## Format bias

- Prefer: <<Punchy+Context | Dense>>
- Avoid: Single-Line Bomb — too little room to handle the trust issue.
- Media attached: <<none | image | video | carousel>>

## Rules to emphasize

Rule 25 (identity disclaimer flip), Rule 31 (transparency parenthetical),
Rule 21 (anti-hype if applicable).

## Constraints

- Do NOT open with the brand/product name.
- Do NOT use "I'm excited to share" / "thrilled to partner with".
- Disclosures belong in the body or in the parenthetical, not buried at the end.
- No em dashes.

## Output request

Generate 8–10 hooks. At least 2 use Rule 31 (transparency parenthetical).
At least 1 uses Rule 25 (identity disclaimer). Recommend top 3.
```

## How to use this brief well

- **Transparency inside the hook = trust before friction.** "(that aren't just another paid post)" does disclosure work AND earns the click.
- **Recommend as "someone like the reader," not as an authority.** Rule 25 is strongest non-expert endorsement there is.
- **Don't smuggle the brand.** Readers can smell it. Putting it in the second paragraph is fine; putting it in the hook kills the post.
- **Examples to study**:
  - Sophie Miller's "(that aren't just another paid post)" — classic Rule 31.
  - Maja Voje's "I'm not a developer. I don't write code. But Claude Code…" — classic Rule 25.
